Biography

Dr. Asif Imran is a faculty member at the University at Buffalo in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering. Prior to joining the University at Buffalo, he served as a tenure-track Assistant Professor in Software Engineering at California State University San Marcos. During his Ph.D., Dr. Imran was advised by Dr. Tevfik Kosar. He was advised by Dr. Kazi Sakib during his Masters. His teaching philosophy centers on student success, and hands on practical skillsthat prepare students for real-world challenges. In research, Dr. Imran focuses on green software engineering, exploring ways to make code, particularly that generated by large language models more energy-efficient.
